Long Road Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Long Road is a Bible app made by Dawid Korecki. In short: we don't collect your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your reading progress, streaks, favorites, notes, feelings, family profiles and settings are stored only on your device. What you type into Verse Maker is never saved or sent anywhere. It is used only on your iPhone to find a matching verse. Photos and voice recordings you add to a verse card or Story video stay on your device, and temporary video files are deleted when you leave the app.

iCloud (sharing the road, if you use it)

If you invite family or friends to share the road, the app uses Apple's CloudKit to store, in your own iCloud account, each person's first name (as you typed it), whether they're a grown-up or a kid, their lantern colour and how many days they've read, plus your sticker counts and sticker-book image if you share them. Only the people you invite can see this, and you can stop sharing at any time from the invite screen or iOS Settings. People who accept your invite add the same few details about themselves. We (the developer) can't read your iCloud data. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Photos

Long Road asks for permission only to add images and videos you choose to save (wallpapers, verse cards, Story videos). It never reads your photo library.

Sharing to Instagram

When you tap "Share to Instagram Stories," the card or video you made is handed to the Instagram app on your iPhone. After that, Instagram/Meta's privacy policy applies. Nothing is shared unless you tap share.

Reminders and widgets

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Widgets and Siri read today's verse from storage on your device.

Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We never see your payment details.

Reporting a mistake

"Report a mistake" prepares an email containing only the verse reference, the narration voice and the app version. You review it and send it yourself from your own mail app to dkorecki+longroad@mail.grokbot.com. We use that email only to fix the mistake and reply to you.

Children

Long Road includes a kids' section. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: dkorecki+longroad@mail.grokbot.com.
